This Privacy Policy explains how Sudoku Recall (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by its developer in California, United States.

Information and analytics

We do not ask you to provide personal information, create an account, or collect information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise location. The App does not include advertising.

The App uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, to measure app usage and understand how the App is performing. Firebase Analytics may automatically receive usage events and technical information about an app instance or device, such as app interactions, app version, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from IP address, and diagnostic or event data. These analytics data are sent to Google and may be processed on its servers. We use the information for aggregate analytics and app improvement; we do not use it to identify you directly.

Depending on your device, settings, and how Firebase is configured in the App, analytics collection or certain analytics features may be limited or controlled through device settings. You can also stop further collection by uninstalling the App.

How information is used and shared

We use Firebase Analytics information to understand general usage and improve the App. We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with advertisers. Google processes analytics information as a service provider under its own privacy terms and safeguards. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

Data retention

Analytics data are retained by Firebase according to the settings configured for this App and Google’s applicable retention practices. We do not maintain a separate database of user profiles. Uninstalling the App stops future app-based collection but does not necessarily delete analytics data already received by Google.

Children’s privacy

The App is not intended to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address below so we can review the matter.
